# Web research

1. Restate the question in one line, and what a good answer would contain.
2. Search with two or three different phrasings. Prefer primary sources: official docs, papers, release notes.
3. Read the pages you cite; don't cite from search snippets.
4. Note dates. Say when something may be out of date.
5. Answer first, then the evidence: each claim with its source link. Say plainly where sources disagree or
   where you couldn't find an answer.

Keep a list of the URLs you used at the end.
